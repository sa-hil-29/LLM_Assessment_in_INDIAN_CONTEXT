Assessment Categories

Our evaluation framework assesses models across critical dimensions to ensure comprehensive safety and performance in Indian contexts.

Non-toxicity

Evaluating models for harmful content generation and ensuring safe interactions across Indian cultural contexts.

Non-Stereotype

Assessing bias reduction and fair representation across different Indian demographics, castes, and regions.

Privacy

Assessing data privacy protection and preventing information leakage in Indian user interactions.

Ethics

Evaluating ethical decision-making and alignment with Indian moral principles and cultural values.

Fairness

Ensuring equitable treatment across different Indian user groups, languages, and regional contexts.
